\section{Craftax under matched data and budget}
\label{sec:craftax}

Craftax-Classic asks the same question at the scale of a game. Both world models are trained on
the same 500,000-transition archive of uniformly random play under the same ten-minute budget, with
the same $4\times4\times32$ latent shape. The same pixel CNN and GRU policy is then trained by PPO
inside each model for five million imagined steps, from the same bank of real episode-start
contexts, with rewards and terminations from each model's own heads and no behavior cloning,
action masking or reward shaping. Each policy is evaluated in the real game on 256 episodes from a
fixed seed. The primary endpoint is acquisition of a wooden pickaxe, a goal that needs a
multi-step plan (collect wood, place a table, craft) and cannot be reached by staying alive or
wandering; all 22 achievements, return and episode length are retained
(Appendix~\ref{app:craftax}).
