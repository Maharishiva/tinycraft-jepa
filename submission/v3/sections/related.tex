\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Joint-embedding world models.}
Joint-embedding prediction avoids reconstructing observable detail
\citep{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa}. LeWorldModel jointly learns encoder and action-conditioned
predictor with a squared loss and a Gaussian regularizer \citep{maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa}
and is the recipe we study; DINO-WM and V-JEPA~2 predict on frozen pretrained features
\citep{zhou2024dinowm,assran2025vjepa2}, and PLDM learns the representation jointly with additional
regularizers \citep{sobal2025pldm}. \citet{sobal2022slow} identify the predictable-nuisance failure
that Proposition~\ref{prop:representation} specializes to the Gaussian-regularized objective; our
matched study adds fresh innovations in the controlled state and finds that the effect of nuisance
persistence reverses under them, a boundary in a different recipe rather than a contradiction.
LeWorldModel itself reports accurate probes alongside weaker planning; Temporal-Distance JEPA,
RC-aux and physically grounded variants add temporal, reachability or task-state structure
\citep{bai2026temporal,li2026rcaux,yan2026psgjepa,liu2026physicallygrounded}, and
\citet{terver2026planning} note multimodal conditional means. Our exact examples isolate the
decision consequences of scoring at a mean and of composing mean transitions.

\paragraph{Generative and distributional imagination.}
Generative world models have supported control in imagination
\citep{hafner2020dreamer,alonso2024diamond,hafner2025dreamer4} and also exhibit perception,
action-conditioning and rollout failures \citep{hansen2026hallucination}; FlowWM learns stochastic
feature-space futures without actions \citep{porcher2026flowwm}. Our flow reference is
action-conditioned, trained on the JEPA's data and budget, and compared through a shared policy so
that behavior, not video quality, is the endpoint.

\paragraph{Objective mismatch and value equivalence.}
That a model good for prediction need not be good for control is longstanding
\citep{lambert2020objective}. Value-aware model learning, expectation models and (proper) value
equivalence fit the quantities a decision class needs
\citep{farahmand2017vaml,wan2019expectation,grimm2020value,grimm2021proper}; TD-JEPA predicts
policy-conditioned successor features \citep{bagatella2026tdjepa}. They are why our mean-state
examples are not impossibility results for deterministic world models, provided the representation
still carries the state.
