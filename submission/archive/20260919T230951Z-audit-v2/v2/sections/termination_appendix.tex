\section{Triggered termination sensitivity}
\label{app:termination}

This follow-up was triggered by the primary experiment's monitored post-terminal weights. It adds
one condition: exact transitions, true termination, and the original learned reward evaluated on
the current latent and outgoing action. It reuses every corresponding reference arm's saved start
indices, checkpoint and data identity, policy initialization, update budget, and evaluation keys.
The MLP stratum adds five policy seeds for each of three models; the native-grid stratum adds three,
for 24 additional runs. The original 72 policies are neither retrained nor relabeled.

All added runs complete 150 updates. All 3,600 recorded training updates have finite required
diagnostics and exactly zero continuation-weighted post-terminal mass and reward. Every final
policy binary hash and per-episode endpoint is checked. Table~\ref{tab:termination-outcomes} gives
the new condition's outcomes. None of the added policies exceeds $50\%$ survival.

The termination contrast changes continuation weighting and removes the learned after-death
extension. The reward contrast holds exact transitions and true termination fixed; policies trained
under different rewards can then visit different states. The latter increases the mean wall count
in all six cells, while survival changes have mixed signs. Both comparisons condition on the saved
encoder/head checkpoint and the given policy/training procedure. The 10,000-resample intervals are
unadjusted and descriptive; three model fits do not establish a model-family effect.
