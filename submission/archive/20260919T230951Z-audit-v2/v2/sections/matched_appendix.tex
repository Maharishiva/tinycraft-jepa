\section{Matched-control protocol and outcomes}
\label{app:matched}
The primary matrix contains three fixed gate model checkpoints, five policy seeds (0--4), and
three transition/signal conditions. Within each model/policy block, initialization, sampled starts,
source identities, checkpoint hash, data hash, requested updates, policy parameter count, evaluation
seed, and episode count must match. Start arrays are compared by semantic content hashes. Only
complete three-condition blocks with 150 finished updates, finite required training diagnostics,
and scalar outcomes reproduced from saved episode arrays enter the complete analysis. Missing,
partial, or invalid runs retain their place in the planned denominator.

The primary endpoints are wall exits before or on the terminal transition and survival
through the 128-step cap. A same-step wall exit counts even if that transition is terminal. A death on step 128 is
counted as death, using episode return rather than duration. Survival above $50\%$ is a secondary
operational threshold, not independent evidence of a specific learned maneuver. Each saved
final policy is evaluated on 256 real episodes with a common key schedule. Outcomes are paired
within model and policy seed. Raw hidden state is used only to reconstruct the simulator transition
source and must reproduce sampled current pixels exactly before training proceeds.

Within a fixed model, policy-seed effects are equally weighted. The main descriptive interval uses
10,000 paired-policy bootstrap resamples with analysis seed 7341, conditional on the evaluation set.
A separate interval resamples evaluation slots jointly across policies while holding fitted policies
fixed. It measures evaluation variation, not policy-training uncertainty. Across model fits, we
show all three effects, their equal-model mean, and range; we do not treat 15 policy pairs as 15
independent models or report a model-family interval. All intervals are unadjusted and descriptive.

Exact-simulator states become absorbing after true termination. When fitted
signals are used, their learned continuation can remain positive there; post-terminal alive mass
and weighted reward are reported as part of the intervention's support change.
