\section{Prospective matched-control protocol}
\label{app:matched}
The primary matrix contains three fixed gate model checkpoints, five policy seeds (0--4), and
three transition/signal conditions. Within each model/policy block, initialization, sampled starts,
source identities, checkpoint hash, data hash, requested updates, policy parameter count, evaluation
seed, and episode count must match. Start arrays are compared by semantic content hashes. Only
complete three-condition blocks with 150 finished updates, finite required training diagnostics,
and scalar outcomes reproduced from saved episode arrays enter the complete analysis. Missing,
partial, or invalid runs retain their place in the planned denominator.

The primary endpoints are walls passed and survival to the 128-step cap. A death on step 128 is
counted as death, using episode return rather than duration. Survival above $50\%$ is a secondary
operational threshold, not independent evidence of a specific learned maneuver. Each saved
final policy is evaluated on 256 real episodes with a common key schedule. Outcomes are paired
within model and policy seed. Raw hidden state is used only to reconstruct the simulator transition
source and must reproduce sampled current pixels exactly before training proceeds.

Within a fixed model, policy-seed effects are equally weighted. The main descriptive interval uses
10,000 paired-policy bootstrap resamples with analysis seed 7341, conditional on the evaluation set.
A separate interval resamples evaluation slots jointly across policies while holding fitted policies
fixed. It measures evaluation variation, not policy-training uncertainty. Across model fits, we
show all three effects, their equal-model mean, and range; we do not treat 15 policy pairs as 15
independent models or report a model-family interval. All intervals are unadjusted and descriptive.

Native-grid CNN sensitivity uses three policy seeds, the same three models and conditions, and the
same final-budget and pairing rules. Its outcomes and bootstrap samples are separate from the MLP
primary analysis. Exact-simulator states become absorbing after true termination. When fitted
signals are used, their learned continuation can remain positive there; post-terminal alive mass
and weighted reward are reported as part of the intervention's support change.

\section{Prospective forecast-readout protocol}
\label{app:forecast}
The three gate models stay frozen. Up to 16,384 training contexts and 8,192 evaluation contexts are
sampled without replacement with seeds 20260919 and 20260920, using matching context IDs across
models. Past context may not cross a reset. Forecasts use the original recorded outgoing actions,
with horizons 1, 2, 4, and 8. Parameters and BatchNorm statistics must remain unchanged during
extraction, and cached encodings are checked against the public rollout implementation.

The three feature domains are real future encoding, autoregressive forecast, and copied current
encoding. Each domain has 512 coordinates. An affine ridge read-out uses mean squared error plus
$10^{-3}\|W\|^2$, with unpenalized intercept and no hyperparameter search. Both declared scaler
analyses are retained: current-training-latent mean/standard deviation shared across domains; and
source-domain training mean/standard deviation for each horizon. Both use a fixed $10^{-6}$ scale
floor, and every transfer applies its source-fitted scaler. No evaluation data determine a scaler,
regularizer, checkpoint, or reporting condition.

Cube coordinates are divided by 63 for fitting and reported in pixels. Wall position is fitted and
evaluated only on truth-present rows; the absent-wall sentinel is never regressed as a coordinate.
Presence reports class prevalence, AUROC, average precision, and threshold accuracy/balanced
accuracy, with undefined one-class metrics omitted. Every task includes a constant training-label-mean
baseline and sample counts. A task with fewer than 64 fitting rows is marked insufficient.

The archive stores reset frames after terminal transitions, so physical-state read-outs use a
horizon-specific continuing-episode mask. In particular, one-step evaluation does not select for
eight-step survival. The hidden-gap negative control instead retains every pre-reveal context,
including later resets, because future-survival selection could reveal the gap through the recorded
actions. The gap label refers to the original current wall. Chance forecasting of this unavailable
bit is compatible with correct treatment of uncertainty.

All fitted weights, scalers, selected IDs, labels, masks, per-example predictions, and provenance
hashes are saved. Overlapping windows are not independent samples; any uncertainty uses evaluation
stream or episode clusters. A weak read-out remains consistent with information loss, poor
conditioning, limited support, or insufficient probe capacity.

\section{Prospective target-gradient protocol}
\label{app:target}
The 12-fit design uses the existing deterministic and stochastic-wind per-frame-gray datasets,
500,000 training transitions each, with three seeds and two target-gradient modes. Every fit uses
8,500 updates, batch 128, AdamW learning rate and weight decay $10^{-3}$, 10\% warm-up followed by
cosine decay, gradient clipping at 1, and the unchanged grid model. SIGReg has weight 0.09,
17 knots, and 1,024 projections. Detachment applies only to targets inside prediction MSE. Frames
shared with predictor inputs retain those input gradients, and BatchNorm/overlapping history can
couple frame contributions. There is no EMA teacher, latent expansion, reward objective, or
privileged training signal.

Paired modes share initialization, sampled windows, and SIGReg projection keys. Checkpoints are
saved at updates 0, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 4250, and 8500; only 8500 is the primary endpoint.
Read-outs use fixed samples of 50,000 training frames and 10,000 independently collected evaluation
frames, selected with seed 19417 without outcome filtering. A raw-latent MLP has two width-256 GELU
hidden layers and two position outputs, fitted for 4,000 updates, batch 1,024, AdamW learning rate
$10^{-3}$, with the same probe initialization and minibatch seed for every model. The primary metric
is mean absolute error per coordinate, in pixels. This differs from the earlier spatial read-out
and its summed-coordinate L1 metric; those experiments are not pooled.

The secondary ridge probe centers raw features, uses an unpenalized intercept, and fixes its penalty
to $10^{-3}\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{Cov}(z))/512$. This is a scalar regularizer convention,
not a transformation of latent coordinates. Constant training-position predictions, RMSE, $R^2$,
latent variance, effective rank, and individual seed effects are retained. The optional 4250-update
probe is admitted for all 12 models or none according to a timing-only gate fixed before outcomes.
Intermediate checkpoints cannot select a favorable final model.

\section{Prospective value-aware continuation protocol}
\label{app:pve}
Complete episodes from the gate training archive are split 40/40/20 into folds A, B, and validation
with seed 417, excluding unfinished suffixes. Probe A is a frozen training teacher; independently
initialized probe B is an auditor never used for predictor gradients. Both are two-layer width-256
GELU MLPs on raw fixed latents, fitted for 4,000 updates with batch 1,024, learning rate $10^{-3}$,
AdamW decay $10^{-4}$, clipping at 1, and 5\% warm-up plus cosine decay. Each must improve validation
MSE by more than 10\% over its own training-fold mean-return constant and have correlation above
0.2. If either fails, no continuation or control claim is made.

The continuation arms start at the same existing seed-0 checkpoint and share initialization,
optimizer, windows, and sampling keys. Both use autoregressive latent MSE at horizons one through
four. The value-aware arm adds
\[
0.1\,\E\left[\left(\frac{V_A(\widehat z_{t+h})-G_{t+h}}{\sigma_G}\right)^2\right],
\]
where $G_{t+h}$ is the unmodified realized return from the future target frame to observed episode
termination, discount 0.99, and $\sigma_G$ is the training-return standard deviation. It is a scalar
loss normalization, not latent scaling or modified environmental reward. Continuation sees A and B
only and uses 3,000 updates, batch 128, AdamW learning rate $10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-3}$,
clipping at 1, and 5\% warm-up plus cosine decay. Both arms calculate the teacher loss, including the
zero-weight arm; actual runtimes are reported because compiler elimination can still change compute.

Only the action encoder, predictor, and prediction projector are updated. Encoder, representation
projector, decoder, and original task heads remain fixed, with parameter and BatchNorm hashes checked.
The teacher and auditor are also fixed. Test data are an independent archive and never determine
normalization, early stopping, hyperparameters, or checkpoint choice. Up to 8,192 shared test windows
from complete episodes are evaluated, conditioned on no reset before their last target frame.

Primary offline comparison is the paired change in the independent auditor's Monte Carlo value
error, alongside its real-latent error. Teacher error, latent MSE, predicted/real latent RMS, and
model-only action sensitivity are supporting diagnostics. Episode-cluster bootstrap intervals use
2,000 resamples and do not quantify training-seed uncertainty. Increased action sensitivity alone
is not evidence of correct action ranking. Agreement between teacher and auditor cannot exclude
shared extrapolation error or off-manifold exploitation.

If the adequacy gate passes, both continuations require the predeclared downstream learned-model
control test: original MLP policy, unchanged original task heads, five policy seeds, 150 updates,
512 starts, horizon 16, and the common 256-episode evaluation keys. The corresponding original-model
policies supply the base comparator. Offline regression improvement does not permit dropping a
continuation arm or claiming control improvement without this test. This is one-checkpoint,
one-continuation-seed, task-supervised evidence rather than a proper-value-equivalence guarantee.
