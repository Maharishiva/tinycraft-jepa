\section{Exact variance omission in two frozen encoders}
\label{app:oracle-variance}

This calculation uses the existing seed-0 grid JEPA in black-background wind as its declared primary
condition and persistent-gray wind as its declared secondary condition. Their independently measured
real-encoding position L1 errors are $0.126$ and $0.717$ pixels, respectively. Those diagnostics motivate
an encoder with accessible state; no probe, learned transition model, task head, or policy is used
in the variance calculation.

\paragraph{Fixed transition law and goals.}
A fresh collection uses 128 streams and 512 transitions per stream, with environment seed 20260923.
From 46,136 past-valid eight-frame contexts, 1,024 are sampled without replacement using seed
20260924. Eligibility for the final calculation requires every action/innovation branch to remain
inside the safe region and before the time cap. This admits the same 843 contexts in both worlds;
181 are excluded for border risk and none for the cap. Every admitted context is crossed with all
25 goals in $\{8,18,28,38,48\}^2$, giving 21,075 pairs per checkpoint. Goal images use the context's
episode texture. No goal or context is selected according to model costs.

Each coordinate of the wind increment takes $-1,0,+1$ with probabilities $0.1,0.8,0.1$.
All nine joint labels retain their original product probabilities, including duplicate clipped
wind outcomes. The simulator state and texture are used to enumerate the exact next images.
Thus the resulting mean is conditioned on the full physical state and texture, not assumed equal
to the mean available from a learned latent history. Current wind is independently verified from
the preceding two positions and action.

\paragraph{Cost decomposition and action comparison.}
For a fixed encoder $f$, goal $z_g$, and action $a$, compute the nine encoded successors $z'_{a\delta}$,
their exact weighted mean $\mu_a$, and
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:oracle-variance}
M_a=\frac{\|\mu_a-z_g\|^2}{D},\qquad
J_a=\frac{\sum_\delta q_\delta\|z'_{a\delta}-z_g\|^2}{D}
=M_a+\frac{\tr\Cov(Z'\mid s,a)}{D},\qquad D=512.
\end{equation}
All arithmetic after encoding uses float64. The independent direct-versus-decomposed calculation
has maximum residual $1.33\times10^{-15}$ and $1.67\times10^{-16}$ in the two checkpoints.
Tolerance-optimal action sets use the fixed threshold
$10^{-10}\max(1,\max_a|M_a|,\max_a|J_a|)$.
The primary disagreement requires disjoint optimal sets. Regret is the minimum exact-cost excess
among mean-cost minimizers, so arbitrary tie breaking cannot produce the finding. All pairs,
including zero-regret pairs, enter the averages.

Regret intervals are $[5.77,9.63]\times10^{-5}$ and $[2.02,6.86]\times10^{-6}$, respectively.
Mean-minus-exact-latent selected actions change expected physical squared-goal cost by
$-0.169$ pixels squared with interval $[-1.107,0.666]$ and $+0.200$ with interval
$[-0.433,0.961]$. Both include zero; no physical-control benefit is established. The discrepancy
is a modest but direct consequence of omitting variance under the specified latent metric.
For this one-step quadratic objective, a mean plus the scalar covariance trace is sufficient;
a full generative model is not logically necessary.

\paragraph{Controls and uncertainty.}
Identity-position, fixed affine-position, and deterministic-branch controls all have zero robust
action disagreements. For physical position, conditional wind covariance is action independent,
so its variance term cannot change the one-step squared-goal ranking. The learned nonlinear encoder
can make latent variance action dependent. Canonical padded inference uses 2,048 frames, with
bit-exact context and permutation parity and unchanged parameters/BatchNorm statistics. The simulator
checks all sampled transitions lie in enumerated support and that all safe one-step rewards are zero.

Goals are averaged within context, then 2,000 bootstrap samples resample full collection streams;
127 streams contain admitted contexts. The intervals quantify context sampling for each fixed
encoder, not training-seed uncertainty. All-branch safety selects an interior cohort and excludes
terminal decisions. This experiment isolates mean-only latent-goal scoring, not survival,
representation sufficiency for arbitrary tasks, learned-predictor accuracy, or recursive mean error.
