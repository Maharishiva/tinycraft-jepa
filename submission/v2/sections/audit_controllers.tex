\section{The gate: separate read-out transfer from control}
\label{sec:controllers}

\paragraph{A task with a delayed observation.}
A vertical wall approaches a controlled cube in a $63\times63$ image. Its gap is sampled at spawn,
at the top or bottom with equal probability, and becomes visible at a fixed reveal point. From the
centre, either gap needs five vertical moves but is revealed only two steps before contact;
retreating creates time to respond. Wall or border contact terminates with reward $-1$, all other
rewards are zero, and episodes last at most 128 steps. Three grid JEPAs are independently fitted to
500,000 uniform-random transitions, with a separate 25,000-transition evaluation archive.

The gate supports two complementary interventions on the same frozen models. First, we refit a
read-out on forecast latents to test transfer from encoded observations. Second, we replace learned
training transitions and task signals with exact simulator outcomes under a common PPO protocol.
These interventions respectively test access to forecast state and the controller-training baseline.
