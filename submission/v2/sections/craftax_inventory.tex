\section{Beyond movement: tool acquisition in Craftax}
\label{sec:craftax-inventory}

The optional Craftax-Classic evaluation asks whether imagination training supports acquisition of
inventory items rather than reaching a nearby player position. The fixed comparison uses the same
pixel CNN/GRU, native action space and rewards, five million imagined training steps, three paired
policy seeds per model recipe, and final native evaluation. Wooden-pickaxe acquisition is the
primary endpoint; all achievements, episode return, sword acquisition and stone-tool rates are
retained. Behavioral cloning initialization and paid-action reward masking are excluded from this
comparison. Model-fit and policy replication, actual compute, and checkpoint provenance must be
reported separately.
