\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Real-transition training outperforms the tested imagination-and-training pipeline on the same
encoders, and at least one frozen encoder supports discovery through a decoder and CNN.
The population objective nevertheless admits control-insufficient Gaussian optima and decodable
state at arbitrarily small loss scale. These existence results do not explain optimization or
finite-network behaviour; the fresh-bit construction does not explain predictable-wall deletion.
The scale diagnostic is descriptive, and the sampling null neither implicates nor acquits averaging.

Empirical scope is one objective, a small gate task, a grid architecture and the reported budgets.
Predictor, head and PPO-protocol effects remain unresolved. MLP results bound neither representation
nor policy capacity; decoded discoveries require off-budget supervision and are confined to encoder
$1$. The flow reference is one checkpoint with different supervision and history, not independent
model replication. Neither non-collapse nor decodability certifies control performance in this pipeline.
