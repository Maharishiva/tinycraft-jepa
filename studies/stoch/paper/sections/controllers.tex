\section{Control comparison}
\label{sec:controllers}

Recurrent PPO \citep{schulman2017ppo,espeholt2018impala,farebrother2024stopregressing} learns either
inside a frozen model or in the real gate. In imagination it observes predicted latents and uses
learned reward and termination heads; evaluation always uses real episodes from reset.
Real-transition PPO observes the same frozen encoder's latents and receives true rewards and
terminations. Both use the same MLP torso design, but real training changes the step budget,
rollout starts, collection length and update organization (Appendix~\ref{app:hparams}).
The true-pixel MLP control shares that torso design, not its input width.

Each JEPA predictor variant has three policy seeds for each of three encoder seeds.
The original predictor and a fitted residual predictor, sampled once or averaged over eight samples,
give $27$ policies in total. The flow reference has six sampled and three mean-of-eight policies
on its single checkpoint. Real frozen-latent and decoded-latent CNN controls each have nine runs;
true-pixel controls have three per torso. Every policy is evaluated on $256$ episodes at its
\emph{final} checkpoint. Walls passed per episode is the primary endpoint; discovery means final
survival above $50\%$. Model fits and policy seeds are distinct replication levels.
