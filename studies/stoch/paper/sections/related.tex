\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Joint-embedding prediction learns targets in representation space
\citep{lecun2022path,assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa}. LeWM jointly trains an encoder and an
action-conditioned predictor with squared loss and SIGReg
\citep{maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa}; other systems plan on frozen features
\citep{assran2025vjepa2,zhou2024dinowm,sobal2025pldm}.
Anti-collapse methods constrain learning dynamics or latent statistics
\citep{grill2020byol,chen2021simsiam,zbontar2021barlow,bardes2022vicreg,jing2022dimensional}.
Our population counterexample has full covariance rank yet no controlled-state information.

Predictable backgrounds can displace moving objects in joint embeddings \citep{sobal2022slow}.
Analyses of self-predictive abstractions \citep{tang2023selfpredictive,ni2024bridging} and
prediction--control mismatch \citep{lambert2020objective} motivate stronger criteria than prediction
accuracy. Reward-, transition- and value-based abstractions supply such criteria
\citep{ferns2004bisimulation,zhang2021dbc,grimm2020value,farahmand2017vaml}.
Our contribution is an exactly Gaussian nuisance-only optimum, a decodable small-amplitude
construction, and a measured pipeline deficit alongside successful control from decoded latents.

Imagination-based policy learning \citep{sutton1991dyna,ha2018worldmodels,hafner2020dreamer}
can use stochastic dynamics \citep{hafner2019planet,hafner2023dreamerv3,alonso2024diamond,hafner2025dreamer4}.
Our flow reference demonstrates feasibility for this task and budget; it does not isolate stochasticity
or compare model families. Likewise, the residual-sampling null is specific to the fitted predictor.
