\section{Objective, task and models}
\label{sec:worlds}

\paragraph{Objective.} The encoder $z_t=f_\theta(x_t)\in\R^D$ and causal predictor are trained jointly
with latent history $c_t=z_{t-H+1:t}$ and matching action history $a_{t-H+1:t}$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lewm}
\Lcal(\theta)=\widehat{\E}_t\|g_\theta(c_t,a_{t-H+1:t})-z_{t+1}\|^2
+\lambda\,\mathrm{SIGReg}(\{z_t\}).
\end{equation}
Gradients reach both predictions and targets, without a stop-gradient or teacher.
For random unit directions $u_p$, knots $t_k\in[0,3]$, quadrature weights $\omega_k$ and
$w(t)=e^{-t^2/2}$, the empirical regularizer is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sigreg}
\mathrm{SIGReg}=\frac1P\sum_{p,k}\omega_k w(t_k)
\left[(\widehat{\E}\cos(t_k\langle z,u_p\rangle)-w(t_k))^2
+(\widehat{\E}\sin(t_k\langle z,u_p\rangle))^2\right].
\end{equation}
It compares projected characteristic functions with a standard Gaussian, constraining the marginal law.

\paragraph{Gate and data.} A red $6\times6$ cube moves in $63\times63$ RGB frames; four actions move it
three pixels left, up, right or down. A white wall three pixels wide enters at $x=60$ every $22$ steps and
moves left three pixels per step; its $16$-pixel gap is at the top or the bottom with probability $1/2$ and is
drawn only once the wall is past $x=40$. Either gap is five moves from the vertical centre, from which the cube
sees the gap two steps before contact, so the surviving strategy is to retreat, wait for the reveal, then move
(Figure~\ref{fig:hero}B). Contact with the wall or the frame border ends the episode at reward $-1$; other
rewards are zero and the cap is $128$ steps. Training uses $500{,}000$ uniform-random transitions, evaluation
$25{,}000$. Random and state-blind alternating actions pass $0.06$ and $0.02$ walls per episode.

\paragraph{Models.} The grid JEPA is Equation~\ref{eq:lewm} with a $2.6$M tile-transformer encoder, a
$4\times4\times32$ latent grid ($D=512$) and an $8.3$M factored spatial--temporal predictor with an eight-frame
history; its encoder is frozen for all later head, decoder and policy fitting. The flow reference
\citep{lipman2023flowmatching,chen2024diffusionforcing,hafner2025dreamer4} is a reconstruction-trained tokenizer to
the same latent shape, a causal latent flow-matching predictor over a $32$-frame window, and reward and
termination heads trained with it. It is one checkpoint trained on the same data and ten-minute budget, and it
differs from the JEPA in architecture, supervision and history length at once.
